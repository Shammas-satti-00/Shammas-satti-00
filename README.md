## About

Computer Science graduate from **PMAS Arid Agriculture University, Rawalpindi**, working primarily in **Unity** across combat systems, game AI, and VR interaction. I'm drawn to the design question behind the code: how a mechanic, an AI behavior, or a level layout changes the way a moment *feels* to play.

- **Building:** *Paladin of the North*, an RPG prototype with custom combat and quest systems
- **Exploring:** AI-driven NPC behavior and neural approaches to adaptive gameplay
- **Goal:** Deepen my design practice through the Erasmus Mundus REPLAY Master's, and work on games with real creative and interactive-storytelling ambition
- **Ask me about:** Unity 3D, C#, VR/XR interaction design, game AI, level design
